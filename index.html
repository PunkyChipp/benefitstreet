    BENEFIT STREET ◆ ESTATE ARCADE
    HOW TO PLAY

    Four weeks on the estate.

    Travel freely between five locations. Each job, favour or break uses one of your two daily turns. Earn rent by Sunday evening. A shortfall rolls into next week.

    Meet the locals, play shifts and invest spare cash in the estate. Each Sunday brings a one-off event. Finish week four with rent paid for the best ending. The game saves automatically on this device.
