    BENEFIT STREET ◆ ESTATE ARCADE
    HOW TO PLAY

    Six weeks on the estate.

    Move with arrows, WASD, swipes or the pad in Lift Rescue. Pick up the fuse, push the trolley onto the striped bay and walk into the lift. Repair three floors for cash and keys. Take the stairs on the estate for a six-landing petition run. Each door offers a different deal. Caretaker keys buy equipment that lasts across runs and seasons. Travel freely between five locations. Each job, favour or break uses one of your two daily turns. Earn rent by Sunday evening. A shortfall rolls into next week.

    Meet the locals, play shifts and invest spare cash in the estate. Each Sunday brings a one-off event. Find Ceefax on the estate, Jan at the shop and Spanner at the laundrette for side jobs. Estate Reputation records the turn you earn each rank. Compare turns with a mate playing their own run. Finish a season to choose a permanent upgrade. Cash, projects and weekly tasks reset. Your upgrades and best milestone turns carry over. Earlier four-week saves keep their original finish. The game saves automatically on this device.
